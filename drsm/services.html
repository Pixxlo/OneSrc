Ten services, end to end

Third-Party Property Management

Our core business since 1983. We run multifamily communities end to end — marketing and leasing, resident relations, maintenance, vendor management, budgeting, and owner reporting — with the hands-on attention that keeps occupancy strong and residents happy.

Property Management Efficiency Evaluation

A clear-eyed audit of operations, staffing, expenses, and revenue at your community. We benchmark performance against four decades of operating experience and hand you a practical plan, whether or not we manage the property afterward.

Asset & Partnership Management

Oversight for ownership groups and partnerships: performance monitoring, distributions and reporting discipline, lender and investor communication, and strategic guidance across the hold period.

Construction & Development Management

From feasibility through lease-up, we manage the development process the way an operator would — because we are one. Our team has developed and delivered over 800 apartment homes across the Kansas City metro.

Property Inspection

Detailed physical inspections that go beyond the walk-through: building systems, deferred maintenance, curb appeal, and safety — documented with the priorities and cost context owners actually need.

Debt & Equity Financing

We help owners arrange debt and equity for acquisitions, refinancings, and repositionings, drawing on a sponsor track record of more than $125 million in institutional-grade multifamily investments.

Financial Workouts

When a property is underwater or underperforming, we build and execute recovery plans — operational triage, lender negotiation support, and a path back to stabilized performance.

Project Repositioning & Rehabilitation

Strategic renovations and repositioning programs that move a community up-market thoughtfully: scoped by ROI, phased around residents, and managed to budget.

Court-Appointed Receiverships

Courts and lenders appoint DRS to stabilize and protect distressed multifamily assets. We step in quickly, secure operations, restore records and reporting, and preserve value for all parties.

General Feasibility Consultation

Thinking about buying, building, or converting? We pressure-test the idea against real operating data — rents, expenses, absorption, and management realities — before you commit capital.
