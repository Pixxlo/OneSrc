LIHTC management

Affordable housing, managed with expertise and heart.

Low-Income Housing Tax Credit communities demand flawless compliance and deserve genuine care. For over three decades, DRS has delivered both across Kansas and Missouri.

Certified & proven

MHDC-certified, agency-trusted

DRS Management is a Certified Property Management Agent with the Missouri Housing Development Commission (MHDC) and has managed LIHTC communities in both Kansas and Missouri for more than thirty years.

That matters because tax-credit housing is unforgiving: a compliance miss can put an owner's credits — the heart of the deal — at risk. Our site teams and back office are built around getting certifications, files, and reporting right the first time.

  • Income certification & annual recertification handled accurately and on schedule.
  • Agency reporting & file audits that keep MHDC and state partners confident.
  • Set-aside and rent-limit compliance monitored continuously, not annually.
  • Resident relations that make affordable communities places people are proud to call home.
