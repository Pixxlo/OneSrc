Apartment management

Our core business since 1983.

Multifamily management is not a side line at DRS — it is the discipline the entire firm was built around. Hands-on leadership, trained teams, and service your residents will remember.

Full-service means full-service

Everything under one roof

When DRS manages your community, there is no gap between "management company" and "the people doing the work." One accountable team covers:

  • Marketing & leasing — pricing strategy, advertising, tours, and move-ins that keep occupancy strong.
  • Resident care & retention — responsive service that turns renewals into the default choice.
  • Maintenance & capital projects — preventive programs, vendor management, and honest capital planning.
  • Financial reporting — clean, on-time owner reporting, budgeting, and expense discipline.
  • Compliance & risk — fair housing, safety, insurance, and regulatory requirements handled properly.
How we run buildings

The habits of a forty-year operator

No. 1

Leadership on site, not on a dashboard

Principals with CPM®-level credentials stay personally involved with every community. Problems get solved by people with authority to solve them.

No. 2

Training as a strategy

DRS puts unusual emphasis on employee training. Well-prepared site teams lease better, maintain better, and keep residents longer — it shows up in the numbers.

No. 3

Service as an asset strategy

We treat customer service as a financial lever, not a nicety. Reputation drives occupancy; occupancy drives value. So we guard both carefully.

Who we work with

Owners, partnerships, lenders, courts

Our third-party management clients range from individual owners to institutional partnerships — plus lenders and courts that need a steady receiver for a distressed asset. Whatever the ownership structure, the standard is the same: run it like we own it.
