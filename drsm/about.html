Our story

Four decades of showing up.

DRS Management began in 1983 as Dunes Residential Services — and has been quietly doing the unglamorous, essential work of running great communities ever since.

Where we come from

Built in Kansas City, for Kansas City

DRS Management, LLC — formerly Dunes Residential Services, Inc. — has made the management of multifamily rental communities its core business since 1983. What started as one founder's hands-on standard has grown into a firm trusted with communities across Kansas and Missouri.

Along the way, DRS and founder Ward A. Katz have sponsored more than $125 million in institutional-grade property investments and developed over 800 apartment homes of our own — so we think like owners, because we are owners.

Today the firm pairs that institutional discipline with something harder to teach: a genuine belief that a well-managed property should feel like home, for the people who live there and the people who own it.

Milestones

The long game, played well

A few markers from forty-plus years in the multifamily business.

1983

Dunes Residential Services is founded

Ward A. Katz launches the firm with a simple thesis: institutional rigor plus genuine customer service wins in multifamily.

1990s–2000s

As principal developer, Katz delivers The Dunes at St. Andrews, Eastland Center, and Falcon Valley — 688 apartment homes across the KC metro, managed by DRS from day one.

2000s

Today
